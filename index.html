How to Play

🎯 Your Mission

Travel through famous Bible stories. Complete challenges, answer questions, and learn how heroes of the Bible trusted God!

⭐ Earn Stars & Points

Answer correctly and complete challenges to earn points and stars. Get 3 stars by doing your best!

🏅 Collect Badges

Finish each level to unlock special Bible Hero badges. Become a true Bible Hero!

🔓 Unlock Levels

Complete a level to unlock the next adventure. Keep going until you finish all the stories!

🔊 Sound

Tap the sound button anytime to turn music and sounds on or off.
